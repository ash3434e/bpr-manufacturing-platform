You can build this project as a smart manufacturing planning platform based on the
concept of Buffer P enetration Ratio (BPR), which is commonly used in Theory of
Constraints  and Demand-Driven Material R equirements Planning .
The main idea is:
Every raw material, component, workstation, supplier, or production line has a buffer.
That buffer is divided into zones like Green, Y ellow, and R ed.
The software tracks how much of the buffer is consumed.
If a buffer is highly penetrated (R ed zone), it means risk of shortage, production delay,
or supply issue.
If the buffer is too full (Green zone always overloaded), it means excess inventory and
unnecessary cost.
